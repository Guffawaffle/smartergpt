The policy surface is what agents can inspect before they act.

Policy is useful when it is concrete enough for tools and agents to read: module boundaries, capability metadata, authority envelopes, and escalation rules.

Policy layers

Repository policy

Lex policy files describe module IDs, boundaries, allowed edges, forbidden edges, and other architectural constraints.

Capability policy

AXF capabilities can describe inputs, lifecycle state, expected side effects, and safe usage notes.

Authority policy

Authority envelopes describe what this run may attempt and what must escalate.

Run policy

LexRunner applies the relevant gates and promotion rules for the current plan.

Working rule

Do not ask an agent to infer policy from company folklore when a small structured file or envelope can state the boundary directly.
